📦 Shipping Policy

We ship RL Shine products across India through trusted courier partners.

  • Delivery Time: 3–5 business days for consumer orders; 3–7 business days for retailer bulk orders.
  • Free Shipping: Automatically applied on consumer orders above ₹999. Retailer and Wholesaler orders ship free as per their plan.
  • Shipping Charge: A flat ₹50 is charged on consumer orders below ₹999.
  • Order Tracking: A tracking link is shared via email/SMS once your order is dispatched.
  • Serviceable Areas: We currently deliver pan-India, including most PIN codes serviced by our courier partners.

↩️ Return & Refund Policy

Your satisfaction matters to us. Please review our return terms below.

  • Eligibility: Returns accepted within 7 days of delivery for unopened, unused products in original packaging.
  • Damaged/Defective Items: Report within 48 hours of delivery with photos for a free replacement or full refund.
  • Refund Timeline: Approved refunds are processed within 5–7 business days to the original payment method.
  • Non-Returnable: Opened or used bottles cannot be returned for hygiene reasons, unless defective.
  • How to Request: Email rlshinebusiness@gmail.com with your order ID and reason for return.

🔒 Privacy Policy

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data.

  • Information We Collect: Name, contact details, delivery address, and order history when you shop or register with us.
  • How We Use It: To process orders, provide customer support, and share relevant offers (only if opted in).
  • Payment Security: Card and UPI details are processed by secure, PCI-compliant payment gateways — we never store your card details.
  • Data Sharing: We do not sell your personal information to third parties. Data is shared only with courier partners for delivery purposes.
  • Your Rights: You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data by emailing rlshinebusiness@gmail.com.

📜 Terms & Conditions

By using this website and purchasing from RL Shine, you agree to the following terms.

  • Pricing: All prices are listed in INR and inclusive of applicable taxes at checkout (18% GST added on subtotal + shipping).
  • Order Acceptance: Orders are confirmed only after successful payment or COD confirmation.
  • Retailer/Wholesaler Terms: Bulk pricing, minimum order quantities, and payment terms are governed by the specific plan selected at registration.
  • Product Use: RL Shine is intended for dishwashing purposes only. Keep out of reach of children.
  • Liability: RL Shine is not liable for delays caused by courier partners or events beyond our control.
  • Changes: We may update these terms periodically; continued use of the site implies acceptance of the latest version.
